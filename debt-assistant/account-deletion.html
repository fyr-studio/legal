Account Deletion Request

Debt Assistant  ·  Fyr Studio  ·  Last updated: July 1, 2026

You can request the deletion of your Debt Assistant account and all associated data at any time. This page explains how to submit your request and what data will be deleted.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Send an email to fyr.studio.hq@gmail.com with the subject line: "Account Deletion Request — Debt Assistant"
  2. Include the email address associated with your Debt Assistant account (your Google account email).
  3. We will process your request within 7 business days and send a confirmation once completed.
📧 Contact: fyr.studio.hq@gmail.com

What Data Will Be Deleted

Upon account deletion, the following data will be permanently removed from our servers:

  • Your merchant profile (name, phone number, bank account details)
  • All debt records associated with your account
  • All debtor contacts you created
  • Your push notification token
  • Your account authentication record

Data Retention

Voice recordings are not stored permanently — they are deleted immediately after AI processing is complete, so no audio data will be retained after your request.

Anonymized technical logs (errors, API response times) may be retained for up to 30 days for system stability purposes. These logs contain no personally identifiable information.

Timeline

Account and data deletion will be completed within 7 business days of receiving your request. You will receive a confirmation email once the process is complete.

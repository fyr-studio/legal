Privacy Policy

Debt Assistant  ·  Fyr Studio  ·  Last updated: July 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Fyr Studio ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Debt Assistant mobile application ("the App").

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: When you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address from Google OAuth.
  • Voice recordings: Audio you record inside the App is sent to our server for AI processing. Recordings are not stored permanently after processing is complete.
  • Business information: Phone number, bank name, and bank account number you provide during onboarding, used solely to generate VietQR payment codes.
  • Debt records: Debtor names, amounts, due dates, and notes you enter or confirm inside the App.
  • Usage data: Basic technical logs (errors, API response times) for app stability. No behavioral analytics or ad tracking.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the App's core features (debt recording, payment QR generation, collection message sharing).
  • To process voice recordings with AI (Google Gemini) and return structured debt data to you.
  • To authenticate your identity securely via Google OAuth.
  • To send push notifications for debt reminders (only if you enable this feature).
  • To manage your subscription status.

3. Third-Party Services

  • Google OAuth — authentication. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Gemini API — AI processing of voice recordings.
  • Supabase — secure cloud database hosting (Singapore region).
  • VietQR (vietqr.io) — payment QR code generation. No personal data is shared beyond bank BIN and account number.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging — push notifications.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with advertisers or any third party beyond the services listed above.

4. Data Retention

Your account and debt records are retained while your account is active. Voice recordings are deleted from our servers after AI processing is complete. You may request deletion of all your data at any time by contacting us.

5. Data Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS. Our database is hosted on Supabase in the Singapore region with row-level security enabled. We implement reasonable technical measures to protect your information.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.

7. Children's Privacy

Debt Assistant is intended for adults (18+) operating a business. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance.
